# 📚 KNOWLEDGE SOURCES RECOMMENDATIONS FOR COLOMBIAN LEGAL AI

## 🎯 **OVERVIEW**

This document provides comprehensive recommendations for knowledge sources to populate the Supabase knowledge base for Colombian legal AI agents. The sources are categorized by type, reliability, and update frequency.

## 🏛️ **OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT SOURCES**

### **Primary Legal Sources**

#### **1. Constitutional Court (Corte Constitucional)**

- **URL**: https://www.corteconstitucional.gov.co/
- **Content**: Constitutional jurisprudence, decisions, and interpretations
- **Update Frequency**: Daily
- **Reliability Score**: 1.0 (Highest)
- **Key Data**:
  - Sentencias (Decisions)
  - Autos (Orders)
  - Conceptos (Legal Opinions)
  - Jurisprudencia (Case Law)

#### **2. Supreme Court of Justice (Corte Suprema de Justicia)**

- **URL**: https://cortesuprema.gov.co/
- **Content**: Civil, criminal, and labor jurisprudence
- **Update Frequency**: Daily
- **Reliability Score**: 1.0
- **Key Data**:
  - Civil jurisprudence
  - Criminal jurisprudence
  - Labor jurisprudence
  - Administrative jurisprudence

#### **3. Council of State (Consejo de Estado)**

- **URL**: https://consejodeestado.gov.co/
- **Content**: Administrative jurisprudence and government legal opinions
- **Update Frequency**: Daily
- **Reliability Score**: 1.0
- **Key Data**:
  - Administrative jurisprudence

  - Administrative decisions

#### **4. Official Gazette (Diario Oficial)**
