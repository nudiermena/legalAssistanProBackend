# Legal Research PDF Generation and R2 Storage Integration

## Overview

This implementation adds automatic PDF generation and Cloudflare R2 storage functionality to the legal research system. Every time a successful legal research response is sent to the frontend, a detailed PDF summary is automatically generated and uploaded to the "legal-research" R2 bucket, with the URL included in the response.

## Features

### 🔄 Automatic PDF Generation
- **Triggered automatically** on every successful legal research response
- **Professional formatting** with legal document standards
- **Comprehensive content** including all research findings
- **User and session tracking** for audit trails

### 📁 R2 Storage Integration
- **Dedicated bucket**: `legal-research` for all legal research PDFs
- **Public URLs** for easy access and sharing
- **Metadata tracking** including user ID, session ID, and timestamps
- **CORS configured** for frontend access

### 📊 Detailed PDF Content
- **Executive Summary** with comprehensive analysis
- **Relevant Cases** with court information and relevance scores
- **Applicable Legislation** with status and amendments
- **Legal Doctrine** with author and source information
- **Professional Recommendations** with priority levels
- **Research Statistics** and methodology details
- **Limitations and Future Research** areas

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### PDF Not Generated
1. Check user_id is provided in request
2. Verify R2 bucket exists and is accessible
3. Check logs for generation errors

#### PDF URL Not in Response
1. Verify PDF generation completed successfully
2. Check R2 upload status
3. Ensure response structure includes pdf_summary_url

#### PDF Access Issues
1. Verify CORS configuration
2. Check R2 bucket public access settings
3. Validate URL format and accessibility
